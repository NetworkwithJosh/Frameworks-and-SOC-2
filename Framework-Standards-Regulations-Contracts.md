Framework, Standards, Regulations, and Contracts

A framework organizes outcomes, practices or concepts. It may be flexible and nonprescriptive.

A Standard establishes requirements requirements or specifications than an organization may adopt or be assessed against.

A regulation creates a legally enforceable obligation within its jurisdiction.

A contract creates obligations between the parties that agreed to it.

A policy is an internal statement of management direction and expectations 

A control is the action used to address risk or meet an objective 

The same organization may be affected by all six at once. Your job is to understand what creates the requirement, what is in scope, and what evidence demonstrates compliance.

So the question is, if we know know what they are how do we classify one 

Assignment: Classify five examples as framework, standard, regulation,contract, policy or control 

Frameworks:
1. The NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0 is a comprehensive, flexible, and sector‑agnostic framework designed to help organizations understand, assess, prioritize, and communicate cybersecurity risks.
2.  NIST Risk Management Framework - a structured process for managing security and privacy risk
3.  NIST AI Risk Management Framework — guidance for trustworthy AI systems.
4.  A SWOT analysis helps organizations understand their internal strengths and weaknesses and their external opportunities and threats
5.  The Balanced Scorecard (BSC) is a strategic management framework that aligns an organization’s day‑to‑day activities with its long‑term goals by measuring performance across four perspectives: Financial, Customer, Internal Process, and Learning & Growth.

Standards 
1. ISO/IEC 27001:2022 is the internationally recognized standard for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an Information Security Management System (ISMS)
2. ISO/IEC 42001 is the international standard for establishing, implementing, and improving Artificial Intelligence Management Systems (AIMS) to ensure responsible, ethical, and transparent AI practices.
