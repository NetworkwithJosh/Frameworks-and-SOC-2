NIST CSF vs ISO/IEC 27001

What's the big difference between both of them?

NIST CSF 2.0 organizes cybersecurity outcomes into six functions: Govern, Identify, 
Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover. It is intended to help organizations understand,
assess, prioritize, and communicate cybersecurity risk without prescribing one implementation method.

ISO/IEC 27001 defines requirements for establishing, implementing, 
maintaining, and continually improving an information security management system.
