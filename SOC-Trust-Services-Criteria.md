SOC 2 and the Trust Services Criteira

SOC is a suite of services performed by CPAs in connection with controls at service organizations.

SOC 2 uses the Trust Services Criteria to evaluate controls related to:

Security

Availability

Processing integrity

Confidentiality

Privacy

Security is commonly included, while the other categories depend on the organization’s services, commitments, system, and report scope.

SOC 2 is not simply a list of technical settings. It considers the service organization’s system, commitments, risks, controls, and supporting evidence.

The report is intended to give relevant users information about the controls and the service auditor’s examination.

Exercise 1: SOC 2 Trust Services Categories


## SOC 2 Trust Services Categories: CloudPay Case Study

### Scenario

CloudPay is a fictional SaaS company that processes online payments, stores customer information, and provides businesses with transaction reporting.

The objective of this exercise is to identify which SOC 2 Trust Services Categories may apply to CloudPay and explain why.

### Trust Services Categories Evaluation

| Category | Initial Reasoning | Evaluation and Key Takeaways |
|---|---|---|
| **Security** | Protect individuals and their data. | Security focuses on protecting systems and information against unauthorized access, threats, and other security risks. |
| **Availability** | Important for the service. | Availability addresses whether systems are available for operation and use as committed or agreed. CloudPay's 99.9% uptime commitment makes this category relevant. |
| **Processing Integrity** | Ensure transactions are processed correctly. | Processing Integrity considers whether system processing is complete, valid, accurate, timely, and authorized. |
| **Confidentiality** | Prevent access to sensitive information. | Confidentiality focuses on protecting information designated as confidential from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. Authorized personnel may still require access. |
| **Privacy** | Keep customer payment information private. | Privacy focuses on how personal information is collected, used, retained, disclosed, and disposed of, including applicable privacy commitments and requirements. |

### Key Learning Points

**1. Security vs. Confidentiality**

Security focuses on protecting systems and information from threats and unauthorized access.

Confidentiality focuses specifically on protecting information designated as confidential.

**2. Confidentiality vs. Privacy**

Confidentiality addresses the protection of confidential information.

Privacy addresses how an organization manages personal information throughout its lifecycle.

**3. Processing Integrity**

For a payment processing company, transactions should be complete, valid, accurate, timely, and authorized.

**4. Availability**

CloudPay's 99.9% uptime commitment is relevant when evaluating whether its services are available as promised.

**5. SOC 2 Scope**

Although all five Trust Services Categories may be relevant to CloudPay, that does not mean all five must automatically be included in its SOC 2 examination.

The categories selected depend on the organization's services, system boundaries, commitments, requirements, and risks.

### Conclusion

This exercise helped clarify how the five SOC 2 Trust Services Categories apply to a SaaS payment processing environment.

A key takeaway is that selecting categories requires understanding the organization's business operations and commitments, rather than assuming every category applies equally.
